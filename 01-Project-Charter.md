
# AI-Assisted Azure Technology Risk & Controls Assessment

## 1. Organization
MapleShield Life Insurance (Fictional)

## 2. Business Initiative
Migration of the Customer Policy Portal to Microsoft Azure.

## 3. Business Objective
Improve application availability, scalability, and
operational resilience while protecting customer data.

## 4. Assessment Purpose
Identify and assess technology risks associated with
the proposed Azure environment and evaluate the
design and operating effectiveness of relevant controls.

## 5. Assessment Scope
- Identity and Access Management
- Application Hosting and Security
- Data Protection and Encryption
- Security Logging and Monitoring
- Backup and Disaster Recovery
- Vulnerability Management
- Change Management

## 6. Assessment Exclusions
- Physical Data Centre Security
- Corporate End-User Devices
- Payroll and Marketing Systems
- Non-Azure Cloud Environments

## 7. Assessment Frameworks
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- OSFI Guideline B-13

## 8. Assessment Role
First Line Technology Risk Analyst

## 9. Expected Deliverables
- Azure Architecture and Asset Inventory
- Technology Risk Register
- Risk and Control Assessment
- Control Testing Evidence
- AI-Assisted Evidence Analysis
- Issue and Remediation Register
- Risk Acceptance Assessment
- Executive Risk Assessment Report

## 10. Assessment Limitations
This is a portfolio simulation using a lab environment,
synthetic data, and fictional business processes.
Findings do not represent an actual insurance company.
